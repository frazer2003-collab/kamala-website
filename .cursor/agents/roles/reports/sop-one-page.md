# Kamala desk SOP — 1 owner + 1 staff

Print and keep by the desk. Source: [process-sop-designer.md](process-sop-designer.md).

## Who does what

| Role | Login | Does | Does not |
|------|--------|------|----------|
| **Owner** | Full staff (+ Finance/Settings passcode) | Morning Home, Transfers/refunds, Finance, prices, catalog, stock setup | Routine POS during rush |
| **Receptionist** | Full staff (`/staff/login`) | Calendar/reservations, Transfers triage, Bills, Laundry, stock receive/waste | Portal as daily main tool; catalog delete; Finance unless asked |
| **Helper (optional)** | Portal passcode | Bills: open tab + **add lines only**; Laundry: **sheets only** | **Clear bill**; Stock; Menu; Stay/Money screens |

## Clear bill

- **Receptionist clears only after payment is in hand.**
- Clear moves stock **once**. Never clear “to clean the list.”
- One person owns each open tab. Do not run bills portal + full Bills on the same guest at once.
- If Clear says already cleared → stop; check Stock/Home. Do not re-add the same sale.

## Helper portals (add-only)

- Spare tablet / housekeeper sheets only.
- Helper **adds lines**; receptionist clears.
- Laundry portal = sheets only; guest bags → full staff Laundry.
- Stock / Menu / Money need full staff login.

## Day rhythm

1. **Open:** full staff → Home cards → Bills.
2. **Service:** one tab per guest/room; add lines as you go; clear when paid.
3. **End of day:** zero open tabs → glance Stock headroom / low list → laundry statuses → **Sign out portals** → owner reads Home/Finance next morning.

## Owner rules

- Menu prices, recipes, deactivate catalog: owner, quiet mornings — not mid-service.
- Set real portal passcodes (treat like door codes).
- One named owner per open tab during rush (full staff preferred).
