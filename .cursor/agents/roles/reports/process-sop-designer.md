# Process / SOP designer — report

Source: [Process SOP designer](8a0d36cf-14e6-486c-acd3-03eae85487a6)

## Verdict

Full staff = receptionist daily tool; portals = helpers only. Biggest risk: bills portal can clear (and move stock) with no confirm.

## Top findings
1. Bills portal over-powered for helpers (can Clear).
2. Clear/delete almost no friction.
3. Two people on one open bill = last-write-wins.
4. Portal session 12h vs full staff ~30m.
5. Default portal passcodes weak if env unset.
6. Catalog/price edits are full-staff, not owner-pinned.
7. Home portal links blur helper vs receptionist path.

## SOP gist
Receptionist clears only after payment; helper adds lines only; one owner per open tab; set real portal passcodes; sign out portals at shift end; owner owns menu prices.
