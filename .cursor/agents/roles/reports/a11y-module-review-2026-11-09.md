## Verdict
The staff UI has solid baseline semantics—named dialogs, Escape and Tab trapping, visible focus rings, reduced-motion branches, sticky table headers, and explicit Rooms/F&B wording—but the highest-risk desk flow still mishandles focus when “Clear bill” reveals payment choices. After hour six, the main error drivers are focus jumping away from the confirmation, 16 px radio controls, a screen-reader-incomplete item combo, and calendar/sidebar targets and labels that are technically compact but tiring on shared tablets. Finance is understandable, yet its long single-page book structure and many 12–14 px secondary lines make repeated checking slower than it needs to be.

## Top findings (max 7)
1. **High — Clear-bill confirmation moves focus away from the payment decision** — **Staff impact:** activating “Clear bill” changes `confirmClear`, but `StaffDialog`’s focus effect depends on the inline `onClose` function; the rerender reruns the effect and focuses the first control in `.staff-dialog__body` (the bill-name field), not the newly revealed radios in the footer. Keyboard and screen-reader staff can miss the required payment choice or think the action failed. **Owner impact:** increased chance of abandoned or incorrectly retried clears, weakening payment-rail and stock trust. **Evidence:** `components/staff-dialog.tsx` lines 37–89 always performs initial focus and depends on `onClose`; `components/staff-desk-bills.tsx` lines 281–379 swaps the footer into the payment confirmation while editable body controls remain first. **Suggested fix:** keep the dialog lifecycle effect keyed to `open` only and store the latest close callback in a ref/useEffectEvent; in `StaffDeskBills`, focus a confirmation heading or the first radio via `ref` when `confirmClear` becomes true. Add `aria-describedby` from the `<fieldset>` to the clear note and no-stock warning. Do not auto-select a payment method.
2. **High — Bill item combo does not expose the active option to assistive tech** — **Staff impact:** Arrow keys change a visual class, but focus remains on the input with no `aria-activedescendant`; list children are buttons rather than `role="option"` elements, so screen readers may announce neither the highlighted item nor its position. This is especially error-prone with bilingual item names and similar stock/menu entries. **Owner impact:** wrong item selection can distort bill totals and stock. **Evidence:** `components/staff-combo.tsx` lines 170–198 updates `active`; lines 200–249 create `role="listbox"` with button descendants; lines 258–278 omit `aria-activedescendant`. **Suggested fix:** give each option a stable ID, render/select it with `role="option"` and `aria-selected`, and set `aria-activedescendant` on the combobox while open; announce “No matches” with a polite status. Preserve the existing 44 px option height and Escape behavior.
3. **High — Payment radio rows are too small for tired tablet use** — **Staff impact:** the actual controls are 1 rem (16 px) and labels have no minimum height or padded hit area. The inline wrapped row makes Cash / Thai bank / Stripe easy to mis-tap when standing at the desk. **Owner impact:** a wrong rail directly corrupts the Finance payment split. **Evidence:** `app/globals.css` lines 3967–3989; payment labels are only inline-flex and radios are `1rem × 1rem`. **Suggested fix:** make `.payment-collected-via` a one-column grid on coarse pointers and narrow screens; style each label as a bordered choice card with `min-height: 2.75rem`, `padding: .65rem .75rem`, and the radio at `1.25–1.5rem`. Add a strong `:focus-within` outline and a checked-state background/text cue that does not rely on color alone.
4. **Medium — TWN/DBL calendar bars become an eye-strain and truncation problem** — **Staff impact:** desktop bars are only 1.55 rem tall with guest text at 0.7 rem (compact: 0.64 rem); the new three-letter prefix consumes the most valuable visible characters before the guest name. Coarse-pointer CSS raises height to 44 px, but hybrid Windows tablets can report a fine pointer and stay in the dense mode. **Owner impact:** slower bed/guest scanning increases room-assignment and turnover mistakes. **Evidence:** `app/staff-calendar.css` lines 1234–1303; `components/staff-timeline-calendar.tsx` lines 441–459 renders prefix and guest as one truncating `<strong>`. Full text is retained in the link’s accessible name, which is a good baseline. **Suggested fix:** split bed code and guest into separate spans, keep the bed code fixed-width (`flex: 0 0 auto`) and ellipsize only the guest; raise text to at least 0.75–0.8 rem. Apply the 44 px bar/row mode at a tablet viewport breakpoint as well as `(pointer: coarse)`, or use `@media (any-pointer: coarse)`.
5. **Medium — Sidebar targets and section labels are undersized for shared tablets** — **Staff impact:** nav rows are 40 px high, locale/collapse controls are 36 px square, and section headings are 0.66 rem. These are usable with a mouse but produce cumulative misses and squinting on a tablet late in shift; the collapsed rail also relies on hover `title` for visible names. **Owner impact:** slower navigation and more “wrong module” detours during interruptions. **Evidence:** `app/staff-ops.css` lines 470–515 and 540–555. Accessible labels and global focus outlines are present. **Suggested fix:** use 44 px minimum for nav links and top controls under `(any-pointer: coarse)` and up to the tablet breakpoint; raise section labels to at least 0.75 rem with `--color-muted` rather than smaller `--color-soft`. For collapsed mode, add a keyboard-visible tooltip (`:focus-visible`/popover), not only `title`.
6. **Medium — Finance books are a long fatigue-heavy scan with no jump navigation** — **Staff impact:** Rooms, F&B, and Combined are correctly named articles, but staff must traverse one long page and repeatedly parse many 0.72–0.88 rem captions to reach the second book or owner total. Secondary source text uses `--color-soft` at 0.8 rem; the token is only about 4.7:1 on white, leaving little contrast reserve for dim or worn tablets. **Owner impact:** morning checks take longer and quiet explanatory lines are easier to skip. **Evidence:** `app/staff/sold/page.tsx` lines 533–760; `app/staff-sold.css` lines 86–140, 346–363, and 595–643. The explicit “Business 1 / Business 2 / Owner only” text means separation does not rely on border color alone. **Suggested fix:** add a compact sticky in-page nav linking to `#rooms-business-title`, `#fnb-business-title`, and `#owner-combined-title`; use `scroll-margin-top`. Raise operational secondary text to at least 0.875 rem and use `--color-muted`/`--color-ink`; reserve 0.72 rem uppercase only for nonessential chips.
7. **Low — Empty desk states are visually quiet but weak as update states** — **Staff impact:** the shared wrapper sets all content, including the heading, to muted text, and has no status/region relationship. When filtering or server refresh changes a populated list to empty, screen-reader staff may receive no useful announcement; visually, the action and explanation can read like disabled content. **Owner impact:** staff may widen ranges or create duplicate records because “nothing here” is not clearly distinguished from “still loading.” **Evidence:** `app/staff-desk.css` lines 891–906; Bills, Reservations, Laundry, Expenses, Stock, Items, and Trips all reuse `.staff-desk__empty-state` without status semantics. The copy itself is concrete and action-oriented. **Suggested fix:** keep the empty-state heading in `--color-ink`, body in `--color-muted`, and name the surrounding list panel with `aria-labelledby`. If emptiness can change without navigation, announce only the transition through a dedicated `role="status" aria-live="polite"` message; do not put the action button inside the live region.

## Quick wins (≤1 day each)
- Enlarge payment labels/radios and sidebar controls for coarse/tablet pointers.
- Separate the TWN/DBL token from the ellipsizing guest name and raise bar text size.
- Make empty-state headings use `--color-ink`.
- Add Finance jump links and `scroll-margin-top` to the three business headings.
- Add stable option IDs plus `aria-activedescendant` to `StaffCombo`.

## Bigger bets
- Refactor `StaffDialog` focus management so initial focus runs once per open cycle and each multi-step dialog can explicitly nominate the next focus target.
- Add automated keyboard/screen-reader assertions for bill clear and combo selection, plus tablet viewport checks for 44 px targets.

## Out of scope / ignored
- Guest booking UI and public-site contrast.
- Database, accounting, and stock correctness beyond the UI error risks above.
- Live browser/assistive-technology testing; findings are from source and CSS inspection.
- No commits or product-code changes were made.
