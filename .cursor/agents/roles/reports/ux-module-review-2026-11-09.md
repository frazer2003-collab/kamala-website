## Verdict
The recent split mostly works: Rooms and F&B read as separate books because each has an explicit business number, accountant-specific copy, and a distinct bordered section; the combined owner view is also clearly demoted. TWN/DBL is useful front-loaded desk shorthand and should stay. The largest operate-mode risks are elsewhere: portrait tablets can put the entire navigation ahead of the work, an empty room setup suppresses the otherwise independent F&B book, and the wiped catalog can lead staff into a create form with no usable category. Small responsive, empty-state, and focus fixes would cut training and mis-taps without redesigning the system.

## Top findings (max 7)
1. **Keep the work above the fold on portrait tablets** — Staff impact: at `<=920px`, the sidebar becomes a normal-height block while all groups and labels remain expanded, so an iPad-sized reception screen can require scrolling past most navigation before reaching Home, Calendar, or Bills. Owner impact: slower desk operation and more “where did the page go?” training. Evidence: the shell switches to one column and `.staff-sidebar` becomes `position: static; height: auto`, while the collapsed labels are explicitly shown again. Suggested fix: at tablet width keep a compact icon rail, or turn the groups into one horizontally scrollable primary strip; do not render the full desktop directory above every page. Files: `app/staff-ops.css`, `components/staff-sidebar.tsx`.

2. **Do not make the F&B book depend on room setup** — Staff impact: after a demo wipe with zero room types, Finance shows only “No room types yet” and hides F&B even if cleared desk bills exist. Owner impact: a valid business ledger disappears, undermining trust in the Finance split. Evidence: `noRoomsConfigured ? ... : <div className="staff-sold__books">` wraps Rooms, F&B, and Combined together. Suggested fix: show the Rooms setup empty state inside Business 1 only; always render Business 2, and render Combined with whatever streams are available. File: `app/staff/sold/page.tsx`.

3. **Make the wiped catalog’s first action completable** — Staff impact: the empty Menu state points to “New item,” but the create dialog’s required category select can have no options when categories were also wiped; staff reach a dead end. Owner impact: rebuilding the sellable catalog needs technical help instead of point-and-teach setup. Evidence: the empty CTA calls `openCreate()`, while the category select is populated only from `activeCategories` and defaults to `"extra"` even when no matching option exists. Suggested fix: if no active category exists, make the empty state’s primary action “Add first category,” or create a safe starter category before opening the item form. Files: `components/staff-desk-items.tsx`, `components/staff-desk-catalog.tsx`.

4. **Make EN/TH scope honest and consistent** — Staff impact: the language toggle translates Bills, Items, Laundry, portals, and navigation, but Home, Calendar, and Finance retain dense hard-coded English. Thai-first staff therefore cannot rely on the toggle on the most scan-heavy surfaces. Owner impact: training must explain which pages ignore the selected language. Evidence: direct English strings throughout `components/staff-home-board.tsx`, `app/staff/calendar/page.tsx`, and `app/staff/sold/page.tsx`, versus keyed copy in `lib/staff-i18n.ts`. Suggested fix: key only the high-frequency headings, statuses, empty states, and irreversible-action copy first; leave accountant explanatory prose English until reviewed rather than mixing translated controls with English action states. Files: `lib/staff-i18n.ts`, `components/staff-home-board.tsx`, `app/staff/calendar/page.tsx`, `app/staff/sold/page.tsx`.

5. **Stop open dialogs from re-running their focus setup** — Staff impact: `StaffDialog`’s focus-trap effect depends on `onClose`; most callers pass a new inline callback each render. State updates inside a dialog can therefore tear down and re-run the effect, restore focus, and then move focus back to the first body control—especially noticeable when choosing stock or changing dialog mode. Owner impact: intermittent focus jumps look like staff error and increase duplicate taps. Evidence: `useEffect(..., [open, onClose])` plus inline `onClose={() => ...}` across desk dialogs. Suggested fix: keep the latest close callback in a ref and run trap/initial-focus setup only when `open` changes. Files: `components/staff-dialog.tsx`, `components/staff-desk-bills.tsx`, `components/staff-desk-items.tsx`, `components/staff-desk-stock.tsx`.

6. **Give an empty Home one clear status instead of six empty cards** — Staff impact: after the wipe, Home presents Bank, Check-in, Check-out, Tours, Deep clean, and Needs reply as separate zero-count panels; the first glance is mostly repeated absence rather than “nothing needs action.” Owner impact: the morning board feels unfinished rather than confidently clear. Evidence: every `Section` renders even at zero, and the page has no aggregate all-clear state. Suggested fix: add a compact “All clear today” status when all actionable counts are zero, then reduce empty sections to compact rows while keeping their labels visible for training. Files: `components/staff-home-board.tsx`, `app/staff-ops.css`.

7. **Keep “New bill” visible whenever staff are operating bills** — Staff impact: once any bill exists, the primary task moves under the generic Actions menu; opening a second tab is a high-frequency desk action and should not require recall. Owner impact: staff are more likely to reuse the wrong guest’s bill. Evidence: the empty state exposes the button, but the populated list toolbar places the same form only inside `StaffDeskActionsMenu`. Suggested fix: keep a compact “New bill” button directly in the toolbar and reserve Actions for secondary operations. Files: `components/staff-desk-bills.tsx`, `app/staff-desk.css`.

## Quick wins (≤1 day each)
8. **Differentiate “catalog empty” from “no filter matches”** — Staff impact: a failed search currently says “No menu items yet” and offers New item, which can create duplicates. Owner impact: cleaner catalog data. Fix: when `sellables` exist but `visible` is empty, say “No matches” and offer “Clear filters”; reserve setup copy for a truly empty catalog. File: `components/staff-desk-items.tsx`.

9. **Keep TWN/DBL, but visually separate the code from the guest name** — Staff impact: the prefix answers the bed question before opening a stay and is correctly placed first; on short bars, however, the combined string truncates as one label and can crowd out the guest name. Owner impact: fewer bedding mistakes without adding a calendar column. Fix: render the three-letter code as a fixed-width, high-contrast token followed by the flexible guest label; keep the full combined label in the accessible name. Do not hide the code on tablet. Files: `lib/bed-setup.ts`, `components/staff-timeline-calendar.tsx`, `app/staff-calendar.css`.

10. **Show combo result limits and complete combobox semantics** — Staff impact: item search silently shows only the first 12 matches, so a broad bilingual query can look exhaustive when it is not. Owner impact: fewer “item missing” reports. Fix: append “12 shown — keep typing” when truncated and wire `aria-activedescendant`/option IDs (or use proper `role="option"` rows) so keyboard and screen-reader selection agree. Files: `components/staff-combo.tsx`, `app/staff-desk.css`.

11. **Shorten the Finance first screen without weakening the split** — Staff impact: the split is clear, but the header repeats “two businesses,” then each business repeats “not the other business”; on tablet this delays the first number. Owner impact: slower morning scan. Fix: keep `Business 1 · Rooms`, `Business 2 · F&B`, and `Owner only`; reduce each lead to one short sentence and place the first total directly below it. Files: `app/staff/sold/page.tsx`, `components/finance-fnb-sales.tsx`, `app/staff-sold.css`.

12. **Put the locale switch on portal surfaces** — Staff impact: portal login and portal shell honor the locale cookie but provide no visible EN/TH switch, awkward on shared tablets used by different shifts. Owner impact: fewer passcode/login handoffs. Fix: add the existing compact locale toggle beside the portal brand, including on login. Files: `components/staff-portal-shell.tsx`, `app/staff/portal/login/page.tsx`.

## Bigger bets
- None recommended before the quick fixes. The underlying list/detail, whole-row click, modal workflow, range controls, and two-book Finance model are appropriate for an interruptible reception product.

## Accessibility / fatigue follow-up
- Verify touch size for `.staff-desk__tab` (`2.35rem` minimum), sidebar links (`2.5rem`), and locale/collapse controls (`2.25rem`); these are below a comfortable 44px shared-tablet target. Files: `app/staff-desk.css`, `app/staff-ops.css`.
- Check calendar type and bar labels at tablet density: room type drops to `0.55rem`, compact guest labels to `0.64rem`, and secondary bar text to `0.62rem`. This is a fatigue risk under glare even if contrast technically passes. File: `app/staff-calendar.css`.
- Re-test muted completed rows: `.staff-home-board__clean--done` uses `opacity: 0.55` on the whole row, reducing text and interactive affordance contrast together. File: `app/staff-ops.css`.
- Audit the combo pattern with keyboard and a screen reader: active visual styling is not currently exposed with `aria-activedescendant`, and buttons inside a `listbox` are not explicit options. File: `components/staff-combo.tsx`.
- Check whole-table-row controls with VoiceOver/TalkBack. `<tr role="button" tabIndex={0}>` makes a large target, but can weaken table semantics; a stretched link/button in the first cell may provide more predictable navigation while preserving whole-row taps. Files: `components/staff-desk-bills.tsx`, `components/staff-desk-items.tsx`, `components/staff-desk-stock.tsx`.
